EXPERIENCED RV TECHNICIANS

A better standard for camper repair.

Over the years, we’ve bought hundreds of used campers and have seen a wide range of previous repairs—some excellent, and some that relied on shortcuts, mismatched materials or temporary patches. That experience shaped the way we approach service today.

Our goal is simple: repair the problem properly, use materials and methods that make sense for the coach, and leave the finished work looking as close to factory-correct as practical.

SLIDE-OUTS

Slide-out repair

Structural, mechanical and sealing repairs with an emphasis on stronger, cleaner repairs rather than temporary fixes.

FLOORS

Floor repair

Damaged and soft-floor repairs completed with proper reconstruction methods—not simply hidden with trim or cosmetic cover-ups.

EXTERIOR

Delamination repair

Repair of separated wall materials and related exterior issues when the structure is a suitable candidate for repair.

HVAC

AC & heating systems

Diagnosis, repair and replacement of RV air conditioners, thermostats, furnaces and related climate-control components.

PLUMBING

Plumbing & holding tanks

Water-system, fixture, drain, valve and holding-tank diagnosis and repairs for tow-behind campers.

ROOFS

Roof repair & replacement

Localized roof repairs, complete roof replacements, sealing work and protective roof-coating options.

INTERIOR

Interior upgrades

Flooring, appliances, furniture and other practical upgrades to improve comfort, appearance and usability.

DIAGNOSTICS

Complete RV diagnostics

Electrical, appliance, HVAC and other camper-system troubleshooting to find the actual cause before replacing parts.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Straightforward service from start to finish.

1

Clear diagnosis

We identify the problem and explain the recommended repair before unnecessary work begins.

2

Fair pricing

Our goal is quality work at a reasonable price, with no incentive to create work the camper does not need.

Reasonable turnaround

Repairs should look repaired—not patched.

We don’t believe a customer should have to accept an obvious patch, mismatched finish or rushed repair simply because the original problem is technically gone. Whenever practical, the finished result should look like it was meant to be there in the first place.

Typical covered components
