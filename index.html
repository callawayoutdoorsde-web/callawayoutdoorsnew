COMMON QUESTIONS

Buying a used camper from Callaway

Do you offer $0 down financing?

Qualified buyers may be eligible for $0 down through participating lenders. Approval, rate, term and down payment are determined by the lender.

Do you accept trades?

Yes. Start with our trade form and send the year, make, model, condition and photos of your current camper.

Can you deliver a camper?

Yes, delivery is available. Call for a quote based on the unit and destination.

Do used campers have warranty coverage?

Warranty coverage varies by unit. Callaway also offers service-contract options on eligible campers.
