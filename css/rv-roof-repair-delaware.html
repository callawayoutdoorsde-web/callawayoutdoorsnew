A leaking or damaged RV roof can quickly turn into ceiling, wall and floor damage if the source is not addressed. Callaway Outdoors handles roof repairs ranging from localized leak repair and resealing to redecking, membrane replacement and roof coating systems.

The correct repair depends on the roof structure, membrane condition and the extent of water intrusion. We evaluate the damaged area rather than assuming every roof needs the same treatment.

If you see soft decking, bubbling, staining, separated seams or recurring leaks, contact us before covering the problem with additional sealant. Photos and the camper year, make and model help us start the conversation.
